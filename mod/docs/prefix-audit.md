# Prefix audit (design doc build step 1)

Generated by `tools/audit/build_audit.py` from the old Relic Forge `PrefixTable.cs` (every entry parsed; the script fails if any prefix lacks a disposition). Data: `data/audit/audit.json` (everything) and `data/audit/effects.draft.json` (catalog for `perktree validate`).

## Result

- **148 prefixes** in the old table -> **96 tree effects**, plus **3 new-design effects** = **99** in the catalog.
- Dropped: **47** (12 magnitude, 34 curse, 1 relic-system).
- Kept: **101** prefixes merged into 96 effects (96 port, none left needing a redesign).
- Scope split across all 99 effects: 82 self, 4 enemy-through-me, 13 shared-enemy.

The published `PREFIXES.en.md` is out of date: the code has many families the guide never lists (potion-use, Vigor, kill/shop, low-HP) and Ironclad's four character prefixes.

## Scope rule used

Design doc section 3, applied like this. When an effect touches several, the **widest wins** (self < enemyThroughMe < sharedEnemy), because the tooltip label exists to warn about the widest reach.

- **self**: only the owner's own state (powers on self, Block, draw, Energy, gold, HP, own cards).
- **enemyThroughMe**: only changes what the owner deals to enemies (bonus damage, damage pings).
- **sharedEnemy**: leaves state on enemies that every player's attacks interact with (Vulnerable/Weak/Poison/Doom, stripping or spreading enemy powers).

## Findings that change the plan

1. **13 companion prefixes were hidden-relic grafts** (a donor relic granted silently so its hooks fire). The design forbids granting relics, so each must be **reimplemented** as host hooks. The donors are listed per effect below; three more (Mighty, Intimidating, Supple) were fixed effects applied on a later turn. This is the main cost in build step 3.
2. **The old mod is all Harmony patches on the static `Hook` class; most of that is unnecessary.** Every model hook this audit names was checked against the real `AbstractModel` and exists. 7 effects (exhaust_dodge, ethereal_save, energy_discharge, vulnerable_resist, weak_resist, frail_resist, doom_defer) need Harmony, at 6 sites: `CardCmd.Exhaust`, `DoomPower.DoomKill`, `FrailPower.ModifyBlockMultiplicative`, `PlayerCmd.GainEnergy`, `VulnerablePower.ModifyDamageMultiplicative`, `WeakPower.ModifyDamageMultiplicative`. `CardCmd.Exhaust`, `PlayerCmd.GainEnergy` and `DoomPower.DoomKill` fire on discrete game events. The three resist patches run per damage/block calculation (including card previews), not per frame, and bail out on their first line for anyone without the perk (see Ground rules). The whole Vigor family looked like it needed a Vigor patch, but the old code detects consumption as a negative-amount `AfterPowerAmountChanged` on `VigorPower`, which is a plain model hook.
3. **Wording and plumbing, not redesign.** The three proc auras (`proc_*`) and the effects that boost other perks need the new `PerkProc` plumbing and relic -> perk rewording; `block_per_relic` now counts relics carried; `gain_amplify` lost its old cost (a bundled enemy-rider curse), which is a balance call left to you.
4. **Three effects could hit teammates** (design category 4): `symmetric_vulnerable`, `symmetric_weak` and `random_debuff` picked a random *player* from the whole party (`PickOne(cs.PlayerCreatures, ...)`, confirmed in the old code) while crediting the owner as the source. **Decided: owner-only.** Solo play is unchanged.
5. **Several 'delay' and 'reduced x0.35' mechanics were balance workarounds for relics** (Mighty/Supple/Intimidating applied late because a min-1 effect could not be scaled down). With per-node magnitudes they are unnecessary, so they merge into the plain combat-start effects.
6. **The old RNG was seeded from the relic id** (`RngCompat.Create(seed + turn * ... + hash(relic id))`) and many effects ran as detached `TaskHelper.RunSafely` tasks. The new host must seed per player and perk, and await effects in order (the old code's own comments record a co-op desync from a detached Max HP loss).
7. **Old numbers are placeholders.** `unit` below is the old prefix's number, kept as a starting per-node magnitude; none of it has been rebalanced against the ~35-45 point budget.

## Decisions made

- **Magnitude prefixes dropped** (Legendary ... Broken, Volatile).
- **Gamble effects with a built-in cost are keystone-tier nodes**: `energy_for_hp`, `energy_for_max_hp`, `replay_grant`, `enemy_strip`, `gold_armor`, `vigor_proc_double`.
- **`curse_count_ramp` counts Curse cards in the deck** (not Relic Forge relic curses).
- **`doom_defer` (Undying) is a keystone**, placed about 30 allocated points from a start node.
- **`enemy_strip` (Eroding) is a keystone.** `symmetric_*` and `random_debuff` stay regular nodes, retargeted to the owner only.
- **Branches:** Frenzied (`vigor_proc_double`) and the rest of the Vigor family go to a Vigor branch in the Regent tree; Eroding stays in Gamble; Resonant (`gain_amplify`) goes to a strength-and-defense branch. Delegated to me and decided: **Discharging** (`energy_discharge`) -> Gamble, as the energy payoff; **Obstinate** (`loss_invert`) -> strength and defense, with Resonant.
- **`forge_draw_chance` (Tributary) is one chance:** +50% to draw 1 card per node. The old second chance (+25% to draw 2) is gone; more nodes of the same effect can reproduce it later if wanted.
- **Relics: the imbue system is removed, and this mod is Relic Forge's successor.** Relics are no longer a delivery route for any of these effects: the mod adds no relics, grants none, and leaves the relic pool and reward tables as the game has them. It replaces Relic Forge, so the two are not meant to run together.
- **Chance effects are not capped at 100%.** Every perk of a chance effect rolls on its own (three +50% perks are three rolls at 50%), and a chance past 100% (from one big perk, or from `proc_boost` / `proc_double`) gives its whole part as guaranteed procs plus a roll for the rest. Only on/off effects and resistances keep a cap. Infinite scaling is accepted; the code only caps procs per event at 100 so a runaway stack cannot become an endless loop.
- **Branch names are internal only.** Branches keep an internal id and name for data and tooling, but the tree UI shows no explicit branch titles.
- **Balance is yours.** The per-node numbers are the old prefix numbers and are not rebalanced.

## Ground rules

- **Performance:** Harmony patches are fine if they are cheap. No patch on a per-frame method (`Update`, `_Process`, `_PhysicsProcess` and the like) without asking you first. Every Harmony site in this project today (`CardCmd.Exhaust`, `DoomPower.DoomKill`, `FrailPower.ModifyBlockMultiplicative`, `PlayerCmd.GainEnergy`, `VulnerablePower.ModifyDamageMultiplicative`, `WeakPower.ModifyDamageMultiplicative`, and the mod's `NMapScreen.SetTravelEnabled`, `OnPlayerVoteChanged`, `OnMapPointSelectedLocally` and `DevConsole` constructor) fires on a discrete event or a damage/block calculation, none per frame. Patches must exit immediately when the player has no relevant perk.
- **Open follow-ups:** whether other characters get a Vigor section, and the keyword gaps, all below.

## Branches (proposed)

A branch is a tree-layout idea. Each effect can carry a proposed `branch` tag in `audit.json`; only the effects you have named or decided are tagged so far, and none of this is enforced anywhere.

### Gamble (general branch, works on any character): 15 effects

Instead of scattering the gambles, one branch gives them a shape: cost nodes lead to payoff nodes, with keystones at the far end.

- **Costs and trades** (pay something now for power): `energy_for_hp` (keystone), `energy_for_max_hp` (keystone), `replay_grant` (keystone), `enemy_strip` (keystone), `symmetric_vulnerable`, `symmetric_weak`, `random_debuff`, `gold_armor` (keystone).
- **Energy payoff:** `energy_discharge` (Discharging). The energy keystones grant bonus Energy every turn, so it fires every turn off them, and it also works with any other bonus Energy.
- **Luck modifiers** (make chance-based perks anywhere in the tree stronger): `proc_double`, `proc_boost`, `proc_reroll`.
- **Mitigation** (new design): `vulnerable_resist`, `weak_resist`, `frail_resist`. Each makes the matching debuff N points less effective on you, so the self-debuff gambles (Exposing, Enervating, Chaotic, Echoing) get cheaper the deeper you go. Place them past the cost nodes: tree adjacency then makes the player pay before they profit.

### Vigor (proposed home: the Regent tree): 12 effects

Checked against the game: Vigor comes from the cards Patter and Terraforming (both **Regent**), Prep Time (**colorless**, any character), the relic Akabeko, and a monster. So Regent is the native Vigor character and a Regent branch fits. Members: `start_vigor`, `turn_vigor`, `vigor_strength_pct`, `vigor_block_pct`, `vigor_vulnerable_pct`, `vigor_weak_pct`, `vigor_energy_pct`, `vigor_regen_pct`, `vigor_gain_amplify`, `vigor_unused_to_block`, `vigor_unused_strength_pct`, `vigor_proc_double` (keystone). `vigor_proc_double` (Frenzied) lives here instead of the Gamble branch because it does nothing without the Vigor-consume perks.

Consequence to decide: the old Vigor perks worked for every character. In a Regent-only branch the other characters lose them, unless their trees get a small Vigor section too. Ironclad's own `hp_lost_vigor` (Retaliating) produces Vigor, so an Ironclad Vigor section would have an obvious reason to exist. `hp_lost_vigor` is not tagged.

### Strength and defense (name open): 2 effects so far

Tagged so far: `gain_amplify`, `loss_invert`. Both react to Strength/Dexterity changes. Natural further members (not tagged yet, say if you want a proposed list): the combat-start and per-turn Strength, Dexterity, Block, Thorns, Artifact, Buffer, Blur and Regen effects.

## Keyword gaps

The validator's native list has powers, `CardKeyword` and `StaticHoverTip` ids. These character concepts are not in it and are left out of `keywords` until the UI recon finds their tooltip source: **Shiv** (a card, `HoverTipFactory.FromCard`), **Orb** (`HoverTip(OrbModel, ...)`), **Stars**, **Osty**. Effects affected carry 'Keyword gap' in their note.
